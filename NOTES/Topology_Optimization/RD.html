    Strong Form PDE

    The reaction-diffusion equation for level set evolution is:

    $$ \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t} = \tau\nabla^2\phi - R(\phi) \quad \text{in } \Omega \times (t_0, T) $$ $$ \phi(\mathbf{x}, t_0) = \phi_0(\mathbf{x}) \quad \text{in } \Omega $$

    where:

    • $\phi : \Omega \times [t_0, T] \to \mathbb{R}$ is the level set function
    • $\tau$ is the diffusion coefficient
    • $R(\phi)$ is the reaction term (typically the topological derivative in LSM)

    Nature Of The PDE

    The equation $\frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t} = \tau\nabla^2\phi - R(\phi)$ is a has two effects:

    Terms Role
    $\tau\nabla^2\phi$ Diffusion Term Smooths the interface — acts like heat diffusion, prevents jagged boundaries
    $R(\phi)$ Reaction Term Drives the interface — decides where material should be added or removed

    Weak Form Derivation

    $$ \text{PDE:} \quad \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t} = \tau\nabla^2\phi - R(\phi) $$

    Using Backward Euler for time discretization with time step $\Delta t$:

    $$ \text{Backward Euler Discretization:} \quad \frac{\phi^{n+1} - \phi^n}{\Delta t} = \tau\nabla^2\phi^{n+1} - R(\phi^{n+1}) $$

    Step 1: Multiply both sides by test function $v$ and integrate over $\Omega$:
    where $v = \left\{v(\mathbf{x})\,|\,v(\mathbf{x})\in H^1(\Omega),\quad v=0 \,\,on\,\, \partial\Omega \right\}$

    $$ \implies\int_\Omega \frac{\phi^{n+1} - \phi^n}{\Delta t} \cdot v \,\, d\Omega = \int_\Omega \tau\nabla^2\phi^{n+1} \cdot v \,\, d\Omega - \int_\Omega R(\phi^{n+1}) \cdot v \,\, d\Omega $$


    Step 2: Split the left side:

    $$ \implies \int_\Omega \frac{\phi^{n+1}}{\Delta t} v \,\, d\Omega - \int_\Omega \frac{\phi^n}{\Delta t} v \, d\Omega = \tau\underbrace{\int_\Omega \nabla^2\phi^{n+1} \, v \, d\Omega} - \int_\Omega R(\phi^{n+1})\,v \, d\Omega $$


    Step 3: Apply Green's Identity to the diffusion term

    Green's first identity is derived by applying the Gauss divergence theorem to the vector field $\mathbf{F} = v\nabla u$:

    $$ \text{Gauss Divergence Theorem:} \quad \int_{\Omega} \nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}\, d\Omega \; = \int_{\partial \Omega} \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n}\, dS $$

    $$ \begin{aligned} \because \quad &\int_\Omega \nabla \cdot (v\,\nabla u)\,d\Omega \; =\; \int_{\Omega} \nabla v\,\nabla v \,d\Omega \; +\; \int_\Omega v\,\nabla^2 u\,d\Omega \\[10pt] \implies &\int_\Omega v\,\nabla^2 u\,d\Omega = \underbrace{\int_\Omega \nabla \cdot (v\,\nabla u)\,d\Omega}_\text{Apply Gauss divergence Theorem} \; -\; \int_\Omega \nabla u \cdot \nabla v\,d\Omega \end{aligned} $$

    Green's Identity

    $$ \implies \int_\Omega v\,\nabla^2 u\,d\Omega = \int_{\partial\Omega} v\,\nabla u \cdot \mathbf{n}\,dS \; -\; \int_\Omega \nabla u \cdot \nabla v\,d\Omega $$

    $$ \implies \int_\Omega \frac{\phi^{n+1}}{\Delta t} v \,\, d\Omega - \int_\Omega \frac{\phi^n}{\Delta t} v \, d\Omega = \tau \Bigg( \underbrace{ \int_{\partial\Omega} v\,\nabla\phi^{n+1}\cdot \mathbf{n} \, dS - \int_\Omega \nabla\phi^{n+1} \, \nabla v \, d\Omega } \Bigg) - \int_\Omega R(\phi^{n+1})\,v \, d\Omega $$
